WITNESS STATEMENT — recorded at Jodhpur police station
Witness: Ramesh Soni, shopkeeper near City Park Gate 2
Date/Time of statement: 14 March, 5:40 PM

I run a small stall near Gate 2 of City Park. At around 4:20 to
4:25 in the afternoon I noticed a boy, maybe 6 or 7 years old, in a
blue t-shirt, get into a grey hatchback car parked just outside the
gate. I did not think much of it at the time as I assumed it was a
relative. The car had a Rajasthan number plate, I remember it started
with RJ 19, but I could not see the rest clearly. The car left
heading north toward the highway.
