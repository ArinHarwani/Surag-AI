COMPLAINT — JODHPUR POLICE STATION, SHASTRI NAGAR
Filed by: Meena Singh (mother)
Date/Time of filing: 14 March, 5:10 PM

My son Aarav Singh, age 7, was with me at City Park in Shastri Nagar
this afternoon. I was talking to another parent near the swings. I
last saw Aarav playing near the park's Gate 2 entrance at around
4:15 PM, wearing a blue school t-shirt and black shorts. When I
looked again a few minutes later he was gone. I searched the park
and the surrounding street but could not find him. He does not know
this area well as we moved here two months ago. I am extremely
worried and request urgent help.
